Install

Ready in a minute

News Hammer comes straight from us, not the Play Store, so Android asks once before installing it.

Download

Tap Download for Android on your phone and open the file when it finishes.

Allow it

If Android asks, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

Install & open

Tap Install. After that, the app tells you when there's an update and installs it in one tap.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription. Stories come from each outlet's public feed and open on the publisher's own site.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. News Hammer is Android only for now.

Do I need an account?

No. Everything works without one. A free account (when available) keeps your saved stories, teams, topics and streak on every phone.

Why does Android warn me when installing?

Android asks before installing any app that doesn't come from the Play Store. You only need to allow it once.

How do updates work?

The app checks for new versions and shows an "Update now" pop-up. One tap downloads and installs it.
